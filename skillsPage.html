AI Usage and Student Performance Analysis

How does students' use of AI relate to their performance in class?

I created an anonymous survey and gathered student responses about AI usage and academic performance. I then cleaned and analyzed the data to explore how grades and other performance metrics relate to AI use.

  1. Collect

    Designed an anonymous survey to gather information about students' AI usage and classroom performance.

  2. Clean & organize

    Cleaned the responses for analysis and organized related student, AI usage, course, and grade data in relational tables.

  3. Analyze

    Used SQL joins and aggregations to compare grades and academic performance patterns across students who used AI tools and those who did not.

Analysis focus: Grades, GPA, and their relationship with reported AI usage.
